# Sweep, 2026-08-21: one new live Lean route, one new Rocq route, and a stable release that landed mid-sweep

A scout of both trackers, the community channels, social media and the adjacent
checker projects, against the catalog's full list of known issue numbers. Six
parallel searches plus a triage pass; 56 raw candidates, most already covered.

**Everything below that says "measured" was re-run here.** Where a claim comes
from a source that could not be reached, it says so and is not in the catalog.

### lean4#14875 — the one Lean route still live on stable

[lean4#14875](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/issues/14875), reported
2026-08-21 by [@nielstron](https://github.com/nielstron), **open**, credited in
the issue body to GPT 5.6 Sol.

A `public class inductive`'s generated recursor keeps a reference to a
declaration the producer saw only through `import all`. That dependency is
dropped from the exported view, so a downstream module may bind the same global
name to something else — and the recursor, *checked* against the producer's
`ClassHidden := False`, is *interpreted* against the consumer's one-constructor
`ClassHidden`.

Measured here on **five** toolchains — `v4.32.2`, `v4.33.0`, `v4.33.1`,
`v4.34.0-rc1`, `v4.34.0-rc2` — twenty assertions, all as documented: exit 0,
`#print axioms` clean, `leanchecker` rejects, and both controls behave. Artifact:
[`KernelDefects/Lean/ModuleSystem/classrec/`](../KernelDefects/Lean/ModuleSystem/classrec/).

It forced a correction to a standing structural claim. `KernelDefects/README.md`
said Rocq's module system has *"no Lean counterpart at all"*. Lean got one in
`v4.31`, and it has now produced **two** proofs of `False` — #14609 and this.

### rocq#22387's comment thread — a second, distinct route

[A comment by Gaëtan Gilbert](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/issues/22387#issuecomment-5357992278),
2026-08-20, is **not** the issue body. The body is the let-in *order* bug; this
is let-in **parameters** not being compared at all.

Reproduced verbatim on 9.2.0: `rocq c` exit 0, `Print Assumptions bad` →
*Closed under the global context*, and **`rocqchk` rejects** —
`Fatal Error: Type error: ActualType` at `cst:T.A.getm_spec`, exit 129. The
reporter states it is not caught by [PR #22394](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/pull/22394),
still **open** as of today.

The checker asymmetry is the useful part: the sibling exhibit
[`LetinOrderSubtyping.v`](../KernelDefects/Coq/ModuleSystem/LetinOrderSubtyping.v)
is *accepted* by `rocqchk`. One subsystem, two routes, differing exactly in
whether the independent checker sees through them. Artifact:
[`LetinParamSubtyping.v`](../KernelDefects/Coq/ModuleSystem/LetinParamSubtyping.v).

## 3. What this sweep could not reach

Stated because a recurring sweep that hides its blind spots is worse than one
that names them.

* **Rocq Zulip is login-walled.** The Rocq developers' primary real-time channel
  was not read at all. Several candidate findings rested on it and were dropped.
* **The Lean Zulip public archive mirror stops around 2026-07-30**, leaving
  roughly three weeks unread — precisely the window of the August wave. This is
  the single largest hole.
* Rocq Discourse beyond one topic; coq-club and mailing lists; conference and
  workshop talks; the issue trackers of the independent checkers other than
  `nanoda_lib`; Mathlib/Batteries; distribution packaging patches; non-English
  sources.
* **Embargoed work.** The Lean FRO declined to publish a working `False` for
  #14576, which is what prompted a community index of affected versions. Anything
  under embargo is structurally invisible to this method.
* **OpenAI's internal adversarial campaign** is credited by name in several 2026
  fixes. Only the fraction upstream chooses to publish is visible.

## 4. Two claims the sweep produced that turned out to be wrong

Recorded because both would have gone into the catalog as fact.

* Several scouts concluded from GitHub's `compare/<tag>...<sha>` ancestry that
  the August batch was `master`-only. **That method cannot see cherry-picks onto
  a release branch**, and it was wrong here — reading the source at each tag,
  with `v4.33.0` as a negative control, showed all of it in `v4.33.1`.
* `nested-nonuniform-param` in the Lean Kernel Arena is not a wrong-accept: the
  arena's own expected outcome for it is literally `either`.
